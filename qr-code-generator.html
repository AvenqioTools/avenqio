What is a QR Code Generator?

A QR Code Generator is an online tool that creates a scannable QR code from text or a website URL. Avenqio's free QR Code Generator lets you create QR codes in different sizes and download them as PNG images.

How to Generate a QR Code Online?

  1. Enter your text or website URL in the text box.
  2. Choose your preferred QR code size.
  3. Click the Generate QR Code button.
  4. Preview your generated QR code.
  5. Click Download QR Code to save it as a PNG image.

Benefits of Using an Online QR Code Generator

  • Create QR codes from text and website links.
  • Choose from three available image sizes.
  • Download QR codes in PNG format.
  • Use QR codes on websites, documents, and printed materials.
  • No desktop software installation required.
