What is a JPG to PDF Converter?

A JPG to PDF Converter is an online tool that helps you convert image files into a PDF document. With Avenqio, you can combine multiple JPG, PNG, or WebP images into a single PDF file. Each selected image is placed on a separate PDF page.

How to Convert Images to PDF?

  1. Select JPG, PNG, or WebP images from your device.
  2. Choose your preferred PDF page size, such as A4, Letter, or Fit to Image.
  3. Select Portrait or Landscape orientation.
  4. Click the Convert to PDF button.
  5. When the PDF is ready, click Download PDF to save your file.

Benefits of Using an Image to PDF Converter

  • Combine multiple images into one PDF document.
  • Choose from different page sizes and orientations.
  • Keep each image on a separate PDF page.
  • Use the tool without installing additional software.
  • Convert images directly in your web browser.
