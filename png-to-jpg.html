What is PNG to JPG Converter?

A PNG to JPG Converter is an online tool that changes PNG images into JPG format. JPG is widely used for photographs and everyday images because it can create smaller file sizes with adjustable image quality.

How to Convert PNG to JPG?

Follow these simple steps:

  1. Select a PNG image from your computer.
  2. Click the Convert to JPG button.
  3. Wait for the converted image preview.
  4. Click Download JPG to save your image.

Benefits of JPG Format

  • Often creates smaller image files.
  • Works with most devices and browsers.
  • Useful for photographs and everyday sharing.
  • Allows adjustable image quality.
