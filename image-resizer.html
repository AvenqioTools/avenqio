What is an Image Resizer?

An image resizer is an online tool that changes the width and height of an image. It helps users adjust image dimensions for websites, social media, documents and other digital projects.

How to Resize an Image Online?

Follow these simple steps:

  1. Select an image from your computer.
  2. Enter your desired width in pixels.
  3. Enter your desired height in pixels.
  4. Click the Resize Image button.
  5. Preview and download your resized image.

Benefits of Image Resizing

  • Adjusts image dimensions for different uses.
  • Helps prepare images for websites.
  • Creates images with specific pixel dimensions.
  • Useful for documents and digital designs.
  • Processes images directly in your browser.
