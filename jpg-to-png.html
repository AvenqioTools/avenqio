What is JPG to PNG Converter?

A JPG to PNG Converter is a useful online tool that changes JPG and JPEG images into PNG format. PNG is a popular image format that supports transparent backgrounds and helps preserve image details.

How to Convert JPG to PNG?

Follow these simple steps to convert your image:

  1. Select a JPG or JPEG image from your computer.
  2. Click the Convert to PNG button.
  3. Wait for the image preview to appear.
  4. Click Download PNG to save your converted image.

Benefits of PNG Format

  • Supports transparent backgrounds.
  • Uses lossless image compression.
  • Useful for logos, icons and graphics.
  • Helps preserve image details.
